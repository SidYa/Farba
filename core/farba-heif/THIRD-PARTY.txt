The HEIC add-on of Farba uses these libraries, unmodified, as separate DLLs:

libheif 1.23.6 (heif.dll)
  Copyright (c) struktur AG and the libheif contributors
  GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0, see libheif-COPYING.txt
  Source: https://github.com/strukturag/libheif/tree/v1.23.6

libde265 1.1.3 (libde265.dll)
  Copyright (c) struktur AG and the libde265 contributors
  GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0, see libde265-COPYING.txt
  Source: https://github.com/strukturag/libde265/tree/v1.1.3

Both are built with only the HEVC decoder enabled (Farba's app/scripts/build-heif-libs.mjs
has the exact options). You may replace heif.dll and libde265.dll with your own builds of
the same interface.
